About the workshop

Trustworthy FL for safety-critical industrial intelligence

Industry is increasingly deploying machine learning in safety-critical settings such as smart manufacturing, smart cities, smart healthcare, and intelligent transportation. These environments generate distributed, heterogeneous, and privacy-sensitive data that often cannot be centralized.

Federated Learning enables collaborative model training across distributed data silos without sharing raw data. TrustFL-SIS focuses on the next challenge: making federated learning credible, interpretable, reliable, secure, fair, and sustainable in industrial systems.

Topics of interest

We invite original work on

Trustworthy and explainable Federated Learning for industrial applications
Robust and fair FL under non-IID and heterogeneous industrial data distributions
Privacy-preserving and secure aggregation in multi-stakeholder environments
Federated time series analysis and sensor fusion for industrial monitoring
FL-powered predictive maintenance, anomaly detection, and process optimization
Digital Twins as a trustworthy FL integration framework
Sustainable and communication-efficient federated optimization for edge/IoT
Benchmarks and evaluation frameworks for trustworthy FL in industry
Case studies in smart manufacturing, smart healthcare, and smart mobility

Submission

Call for Papers

We welcome full research papers of up to 8 pages and short or position papers of up to 4 pages in IEEE two-column format.

Papers must be submitted in PDF format, written in English, and formatted according to the IEEE conference camera-ready style. All submissions will be peer-reviewed and selected based on originality, technical quality, and relevance to the workshop.

Selected high-quality papers from TrustFL-SIS may be invited to submit extended versions for consideration in Expert Systems (Wiley).
